01 / CIRCUIT & LAYOUT

The design, in detail.

The goal was a 10W supply across a 9–18 V input range. The editable schematic and PCB capture the regulator, passive components, feedback network, and connections.

Setting the output

A 90.9 kΩ / 12.3 kΩ feedback divider targets approximately 5.003 V nominal. It replaces an earlier divider whose calculated upper corner reached 5.300 V. The design includes an 82 pF feed-forward capacitor.

Accounting for capacitance

Three 22 µF output capacitors provide 66 µF nominal. Analysis assumes only 33 µF effective after DC bias. That 50% assumption still needs checking against the selected capacitor’s bias curve.

Keeping the sources editable

The board contains 16 components and 36 pins. The supplied verification cross-checks PCB connectivity against the schematic, alongside KiCad electrical and design-rule checks.

02 / ANALYSIS

What the model tells us.

Simulation helps test assumptions. These results describe an educational switching model and do not establish the performance of an assembled TPS54302 board.

The controller is an assumption.

The official TI PSpice model failed transient convergence in ngspice. The six completed simulation runs therefore use an educational switching model with an assumed controller, not a validated TPS54302 model.

The load steps expose that limitation: model output falls to 4.119 V and rises to 5.887 V during 0.5 A ↔ 2 A transitions. These excursions are retained as results, not predictions of the real device.

Model assumptions: 400 kHz switching, 10 µH inductance, 33 µF effective output capacitance, and 2 mΩ output-capacitor ESR. Steady-state measurements use the 7.75–8.00 ms simulation window.

Hardware operation, continuous 2 A thermal performance, loop stability, full-temperature output accuracy, and physical efficiency remain unverified. The temperature-drift calculation reaches 4.847–5.161 V, outside the ±3% target; an unfavorable thermal sensitivity case reaches 172 °C. These are analysis limits to resolve, not measured board temperatures or output voltages.
