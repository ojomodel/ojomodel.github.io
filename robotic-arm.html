WHAT CHANGED

Less bulk. A wider foundation.

The early arm wobbled under its own weight. Testing pushed me to rethink the structure, base, and printed fits together.

Weight & support

Slimmer links reduced unnecessary bulk. A wider base made the newer build more stable in use.

Printed fit

I revised connections with unwanted play, learning to design around physical FDM prints as well as CAD dimensions.

Gripper & packaging

I revised the wrist and gripper geometry and developed a taller enclosure for more electronics space.
